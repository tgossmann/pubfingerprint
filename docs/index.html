A publication profile, not a score

Enter an ORCID iD to see how a publication record is composed: roles in the author list, team sizes, collaborators, venues, countries and topics, how diverse they are, how they changed over time and how topics and people connect. No citations, no rankings, no index.

Everything runs in your browser. The public ORCID record and the matching OpenAlex entries are fetched directly from their open APIs; nothing is sent to us or stored.

A profile describes how a record is composed. Higher diversity is a mathematical property, not a sign of quality, and a focused record is not a lesser one. Please do not rank people with it or combine its dimensions into one number, and read it together with the person's own account of their work.

pubfingerprint · open source (MIT) · data from ORCID and OpenAlex. The profile is only as complete as the ORCID record: works without a DOI or missing from ORCID do not appear. Shared first authorship cannot be retrieved automatically. For full control, download the CSV, correct or complete it, and run the command-line version.
